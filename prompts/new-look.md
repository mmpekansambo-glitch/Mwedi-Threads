# New look (web): prompts and decisions

Issue #118. Built with Claude Code in a cloud session from my Mwedi Threads project (thread "Redesign the app's look and personality"). Phone (#120) and the new page layouts (#119) are separate.

## Prompts I gave

1. Original ask: "Let's work at redesigning the app pallette, graphics and more. It is too AI traditional and is missing personality"
2. Reference images: a staggered masonry board, a Meetup event page, Fiverr and Read.cv pages, a Deputy notes board, the Julienne recipe site, and Calm's glassy rooms. "I want people to feel like they get to meet and chat with their creativity in the application."
3. "I want to build with dark mode in mind. I like mist and moss but still want to ensure we capture that natural dye impact especially the indigo color that is known and associated with Lydia of Philipi"
4. "I hate the icons severely" / "Hate the nav pictures" / "I hat the project cut outs as well though"
5. "i would liek the placehoder to merely be the name of the project as typed, and they can be in whimsical fonts perhaps as placeholders if there is no picture"
6. "have a selection of Yarn ish crochet fonts and yarn ish knitting fonts" / "Also create a selection fo rthe seing room as well"
7. "Use 1 and 2 for crochet, 9 & 10 fro knitting, 13 & 16 for sewing"
8. Landing: "Add some movement to the page in some way that is inviting to folks" / "Let it rotate between three frames" / "Slow the threads down."

## Decisions

- **Palette:** Mist & moss (Fog paper, Slate ink, Night mist for dark mode) with Lydia violet as the brand color and the Sewing room's color. Moss is the Yarn studio's color. Madder clay, lichen and ochre are extras.
- **Type:** EB Garamond for headings, Figtree for body text.
- **Rooms:** each room has its own paper-hill backdrop with a felt texture (moss or violet) under a frosted, see-through nav.
- **No drawings or icons:** the nav is words only, with a dashed running-stitch underline on the page you're on. The logo badge and the Overview doodles are gone.
- **No photos for now:** project tiles show the project's name in a craft font. Crochet: Gluten or Pacifico. Knitting: Silkscreen or Rampart One. Sewing: Special Elite or Playfair Display italic. A project always gets the same one of its two fonts.
- **Landing:** three watercolor frames cross-fade every 9 seconds while three threads (violet, moss, clay) slowly draw across the page. The headline rises in word by word. Everything holds still with reduced motion.
- **Kept:** each Overview stat card keeps its own tint (from #109), now in the new palette.
